Title: Telecom Customer Churn Analysis — Data Cleaning, EDA & Retention Strategy

Description:

Analyzed a 7,000+ customer telecom dataset (38 fields) to uncover why customers were leaving and how the business could retain customers.

What I did:
- Audited the dataset for data quality issues, identifying 30,000+ missing values across 15 columns and resolving each based on root cause (e.g., structurally missing vs. genuine gaps) rather than blanket deletion.
- Defined and justified the metrics used for each business question — including churn rate vs. raw counts, revenue quartiles for value segmentation, and tenure-based cohort analysis
- Profiled churned, joined, and retained customers to surface behavioral and demographic differences
- Identified key churn drivers through frequency analysis and cross-tabulation (contract type, internet service, support access)

Key findings:
- Net customer loss of 1,415 in a single quarter
- Month-to-month contracts churn at 17× the rate of two-year contracts

Deliverables: Cleaned Excel workbook with pivot analysis, formal documentation of methodology and findings (Word), and a stakeholder-ready summary of insights.

Tools used: Excel (pivot tables, data cleaning), data visualization, business analysis
